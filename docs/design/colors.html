Three levels

Color names the kind of thing; brightness says how much it matters right now.

Chip tones

Every tone at each level. Quiet is the tone's fill at 40 % over the chip ground #111317, written in #e5e7eb.

In a column

A figure that repeats down a column is quiet; the tone alone says gain or loss. Bright is left for the one row that is a record.

License classes

Vivid by nature: the color is the reading. The fill carries white digits; the tier color is the text of the tinted variant.

Neutrals — grounds, edges, text

Race palette — graphics

$race-*: traces, gauges, maps and canvas, where a line has to carry on its own. Not for chips or table text.

Flags and LEDs

Imitations of real marshalling lights, fully saturated on purpose. Used by the flag widgets only.
